## Bugfixes

- Fixed a critical bug that caused automatic birthday events to trigger much earlier than intended.
- Fixed an issue where gold amounts could appear with decimals. Pets with the 'lucky' trait can no longer gain fractional gold rewards.
- Pets should no longer accidentally fall through the floor during the Trick or Treat game.
- Fixed a bug where Dr. Banzo would sometimes spawn off-screen in a corner instead of his usual location.

## Improvements

- Updated and improved the hospital visit animation sequence.
