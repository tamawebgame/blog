## New Mini-game: Food Knowledge

A new mini-game is now available in the game center! Try to identify different foods based only on their silhouette.
This mini-game also comes with a new achievement: **Cuisine Enthusiast!**

![New Mini-game: Food Knowledge](images/food_knowledge.png)

## New Mini-game: Picture Puzzle

A new mini-game is now available in the game center! Slide the scrambled picture pieces into the correct positions to complete the image.
This mini-game also comes with a new achievement: **Tilemaster!**

![New Mini-game: Picture Puzzle](images/picture_puzzle.png)

## Reworked Jobs

Jobs have been completely reworked! Pets can now be sent to work for a set duration, with the length of each shift varying depending on the selected job. Once the shift is complete, your pet will return with a fixed amount of Gold based on the work type.

The biggest change is that jobs can now be completed entirely offline. Start a shift, close the game, and return later to collect your pet's earnings. Work now functions as a passive income system, allowing your pet to earn Gold even while you're away.

![Reworked Jobs](images/jobs_rework.png)

## Random Phone Calls

Your friends may now occasionally give you a call!

There is now a small chance you'll receive random phone calls inviting you to hang out or participate in activities together.

![Random Phone Calls](images/random_calls.png)

## Modding Improvements

Added a new **Character Selection** feature to the **Stuff Menu**.

- Change your character's appearance to any character from your installed mods.
- Accessible via **Stuff Menu → Change Character**.
- You can switch back to your original character at any time from the same menu.

![Modding Improvements](images/mod_char_selector.png)

## Additions & Improvements

- Your pet may now **randomly ask to be petted** if you've been taking good care of them.
- Added a new snack: **Takoyaki**.
- Petting is now performed by **sliding your finger across the screen** instead of tapping.
- Completely overhauled the **Phone Menu UI**.
- Added new **Tap Effects** that can be enabled or disabled in the settings.

![Additions & Improvements](images/new_phone_ui.png)

## Fixes

- Fixed an issue causing the **Classic Main Menu** to not work properly with **Functional Shell Buttons**.
- Fixed an exploit that allowed players to spam shell buttons to quickly achieve high scores in the **Pet Grooming** mini-game.
- Other smaller bugfixes and improvements.

![Fixes](images/improvements.png)
