## Added a new lot: Restaurant!



![Added a new lot: Restaurant!](images/restu_ext.png)![Added a new lot: Restaurant!](images/restu_inter.png)

You can visit the restaurant and order food (including rare ones) that will boost your stats even more, so be sure to check it out!

## Animal Updates!



![Animal Updates!](images/animals_indoors.png)

- Now you'll be able to move your animal companions **Indoors** and have them run around in the house and cause chaos!
- Added many new animals including new a new breed of cats, cows, and more!
- Added a new "Feed All" option in the backyard animals menu to quickly feed all of your animals.

## New Gameplay Setting: Skills Affecting Evolution



![New Gameplay Setting: Skills Affecting Evolution](images/skills_affecting_ev.png)

Since skills now affect what your pet's next evolution will look like (by overriding the Care Stars when past a certain threshold) it might make it harder for some players who are planning their evolution purely based on their Care Stars, this setting will allow you to **disable skills** affecting the evolution, and the game will automatically revert back to just using your Care Rating when evolving **no matter how your skills** look like.

## New Outdoor Room Background: Forest



![New Outdoor Room Background: Forest](images/forest_bg.png)

Added a brand-new outdoor background, which will be affected by the outside weather, time of day, etc.

## Burglary!



![Burglary!](images/burglar.png)

When at night and sleep, there is a very small chance a burglar may show up and try to steal up to $100 from you! You can prevent stealing by waking up your pet or having an indoor animal to protect you!

## New Themes



![New Themes](images/theme_lavender.png)![New Themes](images/theme_slateblue.png)

There are two new themes available in Settings > Themes section:
- Lavender
- Slateblue

## Improvements and bugfixes





- Fixed an issue causing save losses, by adding a fallback storage for the save data. Still, remember to regularly back up your save file.
- Added shortcuts between the garden and backyard for easier navigation.
- Discipline gain/lose values have been rebalanced to improve gameplay fairness and progression.
- Updated the park background sprite for improved visuals.
- Updated the market activity building for improved visuals.
- Ghost pets will no longer float during the kissing animation.
- Ghosts on family tree will now show up correctly.
- Smaller bugfixes and improvements.
