## New Mechanic: Favorite Items

Pets can now develop a favorite food or snack from an exclusive pool of potential favorites! Every time you feed them something from that pool, there is a small chance they'll accept it as their favorite.

- Favorites cannot be developed by babies.
- There is a small chance that your pet may change their favorite item when consuming another one that fits their taste.
- Favorite food can be fed as many times as you want without your pet refusing to eat because they're full.

![New Mechanic: Favorite Items](images/favorite_items.png)

## Scratch Lucky Tickets

There is a new **Lucky Ticket** scratch card booth in the mall! Make sure to check it out every day to try your luck and win up to **+$200**!

![Scratch Lucky Tickets](images/lucky_tickets.png)

## Social Media: Messaging

Added a new messaging system to the social media app! You can now type messages and even receive a message back from your friends.

Be careful not to send mean things to your friends, though, otherwise they might not like it!

![Social Media: Messaging](images/messaging.png)

## Improved Gifting Feature

You can now gift **Accessories, Food, and Snacks** to your friends in addition to Items!

They may even wear an accessory you gifted them the next time you see them!

![Improved Gifting Feature](images/gifting.png)

## Batch Cooking

You can now cook as much food as your ingredients allow at the same time, instead of having to manually cook each one individually!

![Batch Cooking](images/batch_cooking.png)

## QOL Improvements

- Improved the visuals of the activities area by overhauling many building sprites and adding trees, bushes, falling leaves, fences, and more.
- Improved the **Garden**, **Forest** background, **Rod Rush** mini-game background, and more.
- Elders can no longer get toothaches because they have no teeth.
- Removed the small chance of failing when cooking with your harvests.
- Underworld Monster and Angel pets can now spawn at all ages instead of being limited to adults.
- Tons of engine improvements and fixes to make the game more stable.

![QOL Improvements](images/new_activities_area.png)

## Other Changes & Bugfixes

- Changed the evening starting time from **17:00 to 18:00**.
- Made small changes to word tables.
- Fixed the invited pet's shadow being displaced during hangouts when the Classic Main Menu is enabled.
- Fixed the petting sound being too loud and repetitive.
- Fixed sound not triggering in the Pet Grooming mini-game.
- Fixed poop rendering on top of the room decorator NPC when redecorating.
- Small UI changes and fixes.
