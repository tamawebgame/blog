## New Hang Out Option!



![New Hang Out Option!](images/hangout_01.png)![New Hang Out Option!](images/hangout_02.png)

You'll now be able to hang out with your friend in various locations and have different conversations!
To get started, Open the *Phone Menu*, Go into your *Friends* list, select the friend you want to hang out with and choose the *Hang Out* option!
You'll be able to hang out in the following locations:
- Your friend's home
- Your own home
- The Mall
- The Beach

If you want to have more hang out spots available to choose let us know!

## Cleaning Activity: Brushing Teeth



![Cleaning Activity: Brushing Teeth](images/brush_teeth.png)

Now you'll be able to brush your pet's teeth in the *Cleaning* menu! Don't forget to regularly brush your pet's teeth to keep their hygiene up and increase their discipline!

## Direct Interaction: Pick your pet up!



![Direct Interaction: Pick your pet up!](images/direct_pickingup.png)

Now you'll be able to pick up and directly move your pet around the house!
Simply click and hold on to your pet for a few moments and you'll be able to pick them up!

Note: This option is only available in the main house area.
