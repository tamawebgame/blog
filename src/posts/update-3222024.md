## Added pet cleaning feature!



![Added pet cleaning feature!](images/screen_bathe.png)

Now your pet will get dirty over time, and you'll have to give them a bath using the new Bathe option in the cleaning menu!

## Added toilet option for pets!



![Added toilet option for pets!](images/screen_toilet_menu.png)

You can now make your pet go to the toilet using the Use Toilet option in the cleaning menu. Watch out for the symbol above their head when their bladder is nearly full!

## Added new doctor and clinic!



![Added new doctor and clinic!](images/hospital_01.png)

Check out the hospital and Dr. Banzo's clinic from the activities menu if your pet feels sick!

## Added social media feature!





You can now message your friends using the Social Media app in the apps menu!

## Stat value rebalancing!





Tweaked and rebalanced some stat values.
