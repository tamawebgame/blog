## Added pet death scenario!





Make sure you are not neglecting your pet for too long, otherwise they might die! 
Baby: 24 hours of neglect. 
Teen: 40 hours. 
Adult: 54 hours.

## Added parent care feature!



![Added parent care feature!](images/stayparents_menu.png)

You can now leave your pet with their parents to take care of them while you're away. Their stats won't decrease during this time.
