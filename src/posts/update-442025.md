## Added Gardening!



![Added Gardening!](images/gardening.png)

Start cultivating a variety of vegetables and exotic plants in your brand-new garden! Visit the market to purchase seeds and kickstart your gardening journey. Remember to water your plants regularly to keep them thriving!

## New Life Stages: Child and Elder



![New Life Stages: Child and Elder](images/elder_child_stage.png)

Experience a new evolution path for your beloved pets! Baby pets now grow into children before becoming teens, and adult pets can evolve into elders after extended gameplay with auto-age enabled.

## Homeworld Getaways: Explore Your Pet’s Home Planet



![Homeworld Getaways: Explore Your Pet’s Home Planet](images/homeworldgetaway.png)

Send your pets on exciting activities on their home planet! They’ll embark on these adventures for a few minutes or up to several hours in real time, returning with boosted stats and unique moodlets.

## Cooking with Ingredients



![Cooking with Ingredients](images/cooking_harvests.png)

Craft your own meals and snacks using ingredients harvested from your garden! Discover 18 exclusive new dishes with special effects—available only through cooking.

## Added Crafting!



![Added Crafting!](images/crafting.png)

Unleash your creativity by crafting new accessories, furniture, and even new rooms! Use exotic materials gathered from your gardening efforts to enhance your home.

## Sell Your Food in the Market



![Sell Your Food in the Market](images/sell_food.png)

Become a merchant by selling your home-cooked food in the market! Regular food items fetch modest prices, but specially crafted dishes yield much higher profits.

## New Set: Collage



![New Set: Collage](images/collage_set.png)

Explore the latest Collage set in the Crafting menu! The set includes 1 room background, 4 furniture items, and 3 accessories to give your room a fresh look.

## Bug Fixes and Quality Improvements

- Fortune teller now displays all possible evolutions based on all care ratings, not just the player’s current rating.
- Added 'Out of Stock' labels to market shops and improved stock system functionality.
- Enhanced visuals in the wedding scene with heart particles.
- Polished the birthday party scene for a better experience.
- Fixed game speed slowdown on high-refresh-rate monitors and displays.
