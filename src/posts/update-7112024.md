## Added cooking feature!



![Added cooking feature!](images/cooking.png)

The cooking mini game has you take 3 pictures using your device's camera to use as ingredients in your dish! After you take the photos, stir the dish until it's mixed!

## Added family trees and generation tracking!



![Added family trees and generation tracking!](images/family_tree.png)

Checkout your family tree from the newly added section in the stats menu! 
You can also see your past generation pets from old runs that have ended. 
Note that only families played from this update onward will appear in the tree. Families from before this update will not be shown.

## Added pet vacation option!



![Added pet vacation option!](images/vacation_sea.png)

If you want to take a break and not worry about 24/7 care of your pet, send them on a vacation for only $250! While on vacation, their needs will not drop, and you can end their vacation at any time!
