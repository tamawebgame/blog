## New Food Delivery App: Snapmeal!



![New Food Delivery App: Snapmeal!](images/snapmeal.png)

Now you'll be able to buy food items that are out of stock in the market, and also some cookable-only food items! Check every day for new items and get them delivered right to your house. There's also a new mission related to the app!

## New Items!



![New Items!](images/retroboy_robotty.png)

Added 4 new items for your pet to play with:

- **Retroboy** (Handheld console)
- **Robotty**
- **Rubicube**
- **Fidget Spinner**

## 2 New Background Sets!



![2 New Background Sets!](images/mush_room.png)

Added 2 new background sets created by the talented **@thunderputz**:

- **Mush-room Set** (+ Kitchen variant)
- **Vintage Orange Set**

## New Furniture Items!



![New Furniture Items!](images/city_set_craftable.png)

There are many new furniture items added! You'll be able to buy many of them from the mall and craft the other ones.

## More Immersive Additions



![More Immersive Additions](images/multi_poop.png)

- Your pet will now poop multiple times depending on how long you haven't cleaned their poop! (Purely visual; health/cleanliness drop rate is not affected.)
- Added many new animations.

## New Improvements



![New Improvements](images/pardis_theme.png)

- Added a new theme: **Pardis**
- Seeds will no longer have a chance to be out of stock when trying to purchase them.
- Added new sound effects for different animations.
- Fixed sound effect delay issues.
- Fixed many UI related issues.
- Many smaller bugfixes and improvements.
