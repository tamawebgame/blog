## School System



![School System](images/school_entrance.png)![School System](images/school_welcome.png)

Now your child/teen pet will be able to attend school!
- Once your pet reaches the **Child** life stage, you’ll receive a special invitation to enroll them in school.
- Pets can attend up to **10 classes per day**, each boosting their skills.
- There are **3 core subjects**, each tied to a skill:
	- _Expression_ – via the **Tune Practice** minigame
	- _Logic_ – via the **Track** minigame
	- _Endurance_ – via the **Skipping Ropes** minigame
	- _Flip Cards_ – a shared game that appears across all classes

## Skill System



![Skill System](images/skills.png)

Now your pet will be able to learn skills!
- Pets can now develop **Expression**, **Logic**, and **Endurance** skills.
- These skills play a major role in determining your pet’s **next evolution**.
- Skills increase through:
	- Attending school classes
	- Engaging in specific daily activities and interactions
- Increase your pet's expression skill high enough and they might even start forming sentences!

## Discipline System



![Discipline System](images/discipline_miss.png)

Your pet now has a mind of their own—and sometimes, a bit of attitude.
- If their **Discipline** stat is too low, they may start **misbehaving** more frequently: refusing actions or throwing tantrums.
- Use the new **Praise** and **Scold** options in the care menu to guide their behavior.
- Balance is key:
	- Over-praising or over-scolding can backfire
	- Poor discipline management may even cost you a **Care Star**

## Toggleable Gender Options



![Toggleable Gender Options](images/gender.png)

- A new **Gendered Pets** toggle is available in Gameplay Settings (off by default).
- When enabled, you can view your pet’s gender from the profile screen.
- You can change it anytime via the **Set Name/Gender** option in the info menu.
