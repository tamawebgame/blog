## Added dynamic day and night cycle!



![Added dynamic day and night cycle!](images/skies.png)

There are currently 4 times of day: Morning, Afternoon, Evening and Night! 
Time of the day is determined by your device clock.

## Added weather system!



![Added weather system!](images/mall_rainy_day.png)

There is rain! Now you might randomly get a raining weather that lasts for an hour or two, the chance is based on the day of the month, with some days having a higher chance of raining than others!

## Added a new room background: Wooden!



![Added a new room background: Wooden!](images/night_sleep_wooden.png)

It has big windows so that you can fully enjoy the new views!

## Added automatic birthdays setting!



![Added automatic birthdays setting!](images/gameplay_settings.png)

If you want your pet to automatically age up when they've reached their age up time (baby to teen: 24 hours / teen to adult: 48 hours) you can enable this feature in the new Gameplay Settings section of the settings menu!

## Added ability to offset your pets bedtime!



![Added ability to offset your pets bedtime!](images/sleep_hours_menu.png)

Now you'll be able to change which hours your pet will automatically sleep in the Gameplay Settings section!

Reworked the park background sprite!

## Added 4 new shell shapes!



![Added 4 new shell shapes!](images/new_shell_shape.png)

## Updated pet sleeping mechanics!





You can now always put your pet to sleep without them refusing during their bedtime (sleeping hours), and there is a new 'Bedtime' badge on the sleep button when it's during their sleeping hours!

## Decreased pets active sleep depletion rate!





Now it takes 4 hours for them to get sleepy (as opposed to 30 minutes from before)
